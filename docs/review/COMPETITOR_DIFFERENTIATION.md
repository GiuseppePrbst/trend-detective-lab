# Differentiation

[NASA Giovanni](https://giovanni.earthdata.nasa.gov/) offers broad Earth-data analysis, custom spatial and temporal selection and plots; its [manual](https://giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov/giovanni/doc/UsersManualworkingdocument.docx.html) describes area-averaged time series. [NASA POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/tutorials/data-access-viewer/quick-start/) provides broad meteorological data, visualization and date selection. [NASA GRACE Data Analysis Tool](https://grace.jpl.nasa.gov/data-analysis-tool/) explores time series and spatial averages for GRACE products.

Trend Detective Lab's narrower value is the guided interpretation of precomputed Chilean regional trends: uncertainty, direct test of a difference, multiple comparisons and contrasting sources in one educational flow. It has fewer variables and fixed periods. No claim of being first, universally easier, or a replacement for these tools is supported; no comparative user study was conducted.
