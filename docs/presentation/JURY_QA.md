# Jury questions — English draft

**How is this different from Giovanni?** Giovanni provides broad data selection and analysis. Our narrower educational flow presents precomputed Chilean regional trends, uncertainty and a direct difference test together. We make no claim of being first.

**Why MERRA-2 and IMERG?** They provide long temperature and precipitation records available through NASA Giovanni; they are respectively a reanalysis and a satellite/gauge estimate, with distinct limitations.

**What supports validity?** Reproducible pipeline, documented changes, simulations, multiple-testing adjustment and comparisons with other sources. These are checks, not proof of correctness.

**Why change methods?** Simulations indicated too many false positives from earlier monthly tests. The annual method reduced them in tested scenarios but did not meet the strict target.

**What does non-significant mean?** The test did not find sufficient evidence under its assumptions; it does not prove no change.

**Why do IMERG and ERA5 disagree?** They use different products, spatial representations and inputs. We cannot infer a specific cause from the comparison. Stations measure points; ERA5 checks use five locations.

**How does it support education?** Guided cases connect a question, trend, interval, direct comparison and limitations. Learning outcomes remain untested.

**Can it work elsewhere?** The approach could be adapted with appropriate boundaries, data, processing and validation; the published version covers Chile.

**Can users choose time periods?** Not in the inspected version. Each product uses its published full period; there is no interactive recalculation.

**Team contribution and AI?** The team assembled the analysis, workflow and presentation. Claude and Codex assisted code and documentation. Members must verify their own roles and the final disclosure.

**What works today?** Two variables, sixteen regions, fixed periods, time series, precomputed trend and pairwise tests, selected external checks and CSV download. Recheck the public deployment before presenting.
