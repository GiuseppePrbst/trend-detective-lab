## Selected

1. **Spatial heterogeneity — Antofagasta coast vs interior** (A). In MERRA-2 the coastal cells show no warming while the
   interior warms ~0.27 °C/decade; ERA5 shows the same contrast and the coastal station agrees with the coast. The
   difference survives an elevation-range-restricted comparison and nearest-elevation pairing (imperfect: gaps remain). Coast and elevation remain correlated; no cause is claimed.
2. **Multivariable convergence — central Chile** (B). Detrended year-to-year anomalies of IMERG rainfall and GLDAS
   root-zone soil moisture correlate at r ≈ 0.8 from Valparaíso to Maule; rainfall–evapotranspiration coupling is strong in
   the north and centre and vanishes from La Araucanía southward — consistent with water-limited vs energy-limited regimes
   (interpretation). Caveat: GLDAS is driven by observed rainfall.
3. **Discrepancy between products — Magallanes** (C). MERRA-2 shows no warming and IMERG significant drying; ERA5 and the
   Punta Arenas station show warming and no clear drying.

Not selected: D (descriptive only; land cover is a snapshot and GLDAS does not use it) and E (2000–2024 precipitation
trends depend on the start year: 4 regions with p < 0.05 for 1998–2024, 11 for 2000–2024).
